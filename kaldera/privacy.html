Privacy Policy

This policy describes the data the Kaldera game really collects and processes. It follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for players in the EU/EEA and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) for players in Türkiye. Türkçe metin.

1. Controller

Nurullah Mencik (personal project, Türkiye). Contact: nurullahmencik42@gmail.com.

2. How you sign in

You sign in with your Google account only. Google checks who you are and gives the game a sign-in token; our server checks that token itself against Google's published keys (only while those keys cannot be fetched does it send the token to Google to check it) and then keeps only the stable user number Google issues for your account. The token also carries your e-mail address, name and profile picture address: they are read only to check the token, and are never stored on our server. No password or phone number is asked for.

On your first sign-in you choose a username. It is your account's name on every character, in chat, friends lists and Market listings, so do not use your real name. An account created with a nickname during the test phase can be linked to a Google account once; the old nickname then becomes the username.

3. How data is collected

  • Automatically, by electronic means, through the app and the game server while you play: your actions in the game, the connection (IP address) and the records described below.
  • From you: the username you choose, the chat lines you send, your Market listings and the reports you file.
  • From Google: the stable user number at sign-in (Google sign-in) and, when you buy something, the order number and its status (Google Play).
  • On your device: the app's settings, stored only there.

No precise location, contacts, photos, files or microphone data are collected. Payments are taken and processed by Google Play; your card or payment details never reach us, we keep only the entitlement and the order number. There is no direct trading between players; items change hands only in the in-game Market, for game gold.

Chat and moderation. Before you first use chat or list an item in the Market, you are asked to accept the Terms of Use. Chat has three channels, and the one you select decides both the messages you see and where yours go: Friends (only your online friends), Nearby (players within 40 m) and General (every player in the game at that moment; from level 5, one message per 5 seconds). Your friends see whether you are online, your class, level and the region you are in. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted: the server delivers them, masks known swear words (***) and writes them to the chat log above; do not share personal information in chat. You can block a player (their messages, friend requests, party and duel invites no longer reach you; blocking also ends a friendship) and report them. Only the developer reviews reports and the chat log; accounts that break the rules can be muted or banned for a time or permanently, and Market listings that break the rules can be removed (the item goes back to the seller's mailbox). The chat log is handed to the authorities only where the law requires it.

5. Ads: legal basis and transfer to Google

Personalised ads are shown only with your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a), KVKK Art. 5(1)), given in Google's consent form when the app first starts; the form is shown in the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland. Without that consent, and in every other country (including Türkiye), the app asks Google for non-personalised ads only. For non-personalised ads Google still uses the advertising ID and the IP address to limit how often an ad is shown, to count views and clicks and to detect fraud; this serves our legitimate interest in keeping the game free (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f), KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).

You can change your consent in the game under Settings → Store → Ad privacy options (where the form is shown), and reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings (Google → Ads). Buying Remove Ads or Premium turns off the ads between play.

The ad data goes to Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC, which may process it outside Türkiye and the EU, including in the United States. This transfer abroad relies on the standard contractual clauses Google offers (GDPR Art. 46, KVKK Art. 9) and, for personalised ads, also on your explicit consent.

6. Where data is stored and who else sees it

Hosting today: the game server runs on the developer's own computer in Türkiye, and the account data and its backups are kept on that computer. Connections from the internet reach it through a network tunnel service (playit.gg), whose relay servers, which may be outside Türkiye, see your IP address in order to forward the traffic; the game traffic is encrypted (DTLS), so the tunnel cannot read it. Because of the tunnel, the server usually sees the tunnel's local address (127.0.0.1) instead of your IP address, and that address is what the chat and sign-in log then holds. Later the server moves to a data centre in Istanbul (Türkiye) and the tunnel is no longer used; this paragraph will be updated then.

Turkish and international players play on the same server, so the data of players from the EU and other countries is processed in Türkiye as well. This is necessary to provide the game you asked for (GDPR Art. 49(1)(b)).

  • Google sign-in (Google LLC): verifies your identity under Google's privacy policy. You can remove Kaldera's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Google Play (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC): purchases are made under Google's terms; to verify a purchase the server sends the product and the purchase token to the Google Play Developer API.
  • Google AdMob: section 5.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA): this website is hosted there. Each time the app starts it downloads the server list (servers.json) and the news (news.json) from it. GitHub receives your IP address and device information with each request and may keep it in its logs for security under its own privacy statement; we do not receive these logs.

Data is never sold and is not shared with third parties beyond the above; it is disclosed to authorities only where the law requires it. We do not use your account data for advertising.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR Articles 15–22 and KVKK Article 11 you may ask whether your data is processed, access it, have it corrected or erased, restrict or object to processing, withdraw a consent you gave, and receive your data in a portable format. Send requests to nurullahmencik42@gmail.com with your username and the class and level of one of your characters. Requests are answered within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority in the EU, or to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK Kurulu).

8. Account deletion

You can delete your account in the game (Menu → Settings → Account → Delete account, confirmed by signing in with Google again) or by e-mail, as described on the account deletion page. Signing in again with the same Google account within 7 days offers to restore it; after 7 days the account and all its game data are permanently removed from the server. The records listed there under "Data kept for a while" (backups, logs, reports, the market audit log) are removed when their retention period ends.

9. Children

Kaldera is for players aged 13 and over. If the age of digital consent in your country is higher (13–16 in the EU) and you are below it, you need the consent of a parent or guardian.
