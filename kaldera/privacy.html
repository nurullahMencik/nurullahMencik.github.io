Privacy Policy

This policy describes the data the Kaldera game really collects and processes today. It follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for players in the EU/EEA and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) for players in Türkiye. Türkçe metin.

1. Controller

Nurullah Mencik (personal project, Türkiye). Contact: nurullahmencik42@gmail.com.

2. How you sign in

Today (test phase): you enter the game with a nickname you choose; no password, e-mail address or phone number is asked for. Your account is identified by that nickname, and anyone who knows it can enter the same account, so do not use your real name or the name of another account as your nickname.

Later: sign-in will use your Google account. The server will then keep only the stable user number Google issues; your e-mail address and name will not be stored on our server. This page is updated before that change ships.

No location, contacts, photos, microphone, advertising ID or payment data are collected. The app has no ads and no real-money purchases. Today the game has no chat, market or trading; when they are added (for example chat messages and report records), this policy is updated first.

4. Where data is stored and transfers

The game server, account data and backups are kept in Türkiye. Turkish and international players play on the same server, so the data of players from the EU and other countries is processed in Türkiye as well. This is necessary to provide the game you asked for (GDPR Art. 49(1)(b)).

During the test phase, a connection over the internet may pass through a network tunnel service (playit.gg), which sees your IP address in order to forward the traffic and does not store your game data. Before the public release the server moves to a data centre in Istanbul and the tunnel is no longer used. When Google sign-in arrives, Google LLC verifies your identity under its own privacy policy.

Data is never sold, used for advertising or shared with third parties; it is disclosed to authorities only where the law requires it.

5. Your rights

Under GDPR Articles 15–22 and KVKK Article 11 you may ask whether your data is processed, access it, have it corrected or erased, restrict or object to processing, and receive it in a portable format. Send requests to nurullahmencik42@gmail.com with your nickname and one of your character names. Requests are answered within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority in the EU, or to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK Kurulu).

6. Account deletion

You can delete your account in the game (Menu → Settings → Account → Delete account) or by e-mail as described on the account deletion page. A deleted account can be restored by signing in again within 7 days; after 7 days the account and all its game data are permanently removed from the server, and copies in backups are removed automatically within 60 days at the latest.

7. Children

Kaldera is for players aged 13 and over. If the age of digital consent in your country is higher (13–16 in the EU) and you are below it, you need the consent of a parent or guardian.

8. Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page; important changes are announced in the game.
