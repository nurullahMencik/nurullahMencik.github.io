Privacy Policy

This policy describes the data the Kaldera game really collects and processes today. It follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for players in the EU/EEA and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) for players in Türkiye. Türkçe metin.

1. Controller

Nurullah Mencik (personal project, Türkiye). Contact: nurullahmencik42@gmail.com.

2. How you sign in

Today (test phase): you enter the game with a nickname you choose; no password, e-mail address or phone number is asked for. Your account is identified by that nickname, and anyone who knows it can enter the same account, so do not use your real name or the name of another account as your nickname.

Google sign-in (when the server enables it): the server keeps only the stable user number Google issues; your e-mail address and name are not stored on our server. On the first Google sign-in you choose a username; it is your account's name on every character, in chat, friends lists and market listings. A nickname account from the test phase can be moved to your Google account once (the old nickname becomes the username).

No location, contacts, photos or microphone data are collected. Payments are taken and processed by Google Play; your card or payment details never reach us, we keep only the entitlement and the order number. Only Google AdMob uses the advertising ID; you can reset or delete it in Android settings (Google → Ads). You can change your ad consent in the game under Settings → Store → Ad privacy options (in the countries where the consent form is shown). There is no direct trading between players; items change hands only in the in-game Market, for game gold.

Chat and moderation. Before you first use chat or list an item in the Market, you are asked to accept the Terms of Use. Chat has three channels, and the one you select decides both the messages you see and where yours go: Friends (only your online friends), Nearby (players within 40 m) and General (every player in the game at that moment; from level 5, one message per 5 seconds). Your friends list belongs to your account: your friends see whether you are online, your class, level and the region you are in. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted: the server delivers them, masks known swear words (***) and writes them to the chat log above; do not share personal information in chat. You can block a player (their messages, friend requests, party and duel invites no longer reach you; blocking also ends a friendship) and report them. Only the developer reviews reports and the chat log; accounts that break the rules can be muted or banned for a time or permanently. The chat log is handed to the authorities only where the law requires it. During the test phase, when your connection goes through the network tunnel, the server usually sees the tunnel agent's address on the server machine (127.0.0.1) instead of yours, and that address is what the chat log holds. Market listings that break the rules can be removed too (the item goes back to the seller's mailbox).

4. Where data is stored and transfers

The game server, account data and backups are kept in Türkiye. Turkish and international players play on the same server, so the data of players from the EU and other countries is processed in Türkiye as well. This is necessary to provide the game you asked for (GDPR Art. 49(1)(b)).

During the test phase, a connection over the internet may pass through a network tunnel service (playit.gg), which sees your IP address in order to forward the traffic and does not store your game data. Before the public release the server moves to a data centre in Istanbul and the tunnel is no longer used. When Google sign-in arrives, Google LLC verifies your identity under its own privacy policy.

Purchases are made through Google Play (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) under Google's terms; to verify a purchase the server sends the product and the purchase token to the Google Play Developer API. Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC); Google may process this data outside Türkiye and the EU under its own safeguards (GDPR Art. 46, KVKK Art. 9).

Data is never sold and is not shared with third parties beyond the above; it is disclosed to authorities only where the law requires it. We do not use your account data for advertising.

5. Your rights

Under GDPR Articles 15–22 and KVKK Article 11 you may ask whether your data is processed, access it, have it corrected or erased, restrict or object to processing, and receive it in a portable format. Send requests to nurullahmencik42@gmail.com with your nickname and the class and level of one of your characters. Requests are answered within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority in the EU, or to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK Kurulu).

6. Account deletion

You can delete your account in the game (Menu → Settings → Account → Delete account) or by e-mail as described on the account deletion page. A deleted account can be restored by signing in again within 7 days; after 7 days the account and all its game data are permanently removed from the server, and copies in backups are removed automatically within 60 days at the latest.

7. Children

Kaldera is for players aged 13 and over. If the age of digital consent in your country is higher (13–16 in the EU) and you are below it, you need the consent of a parent or guardian.

8. Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page; important changes are announced in the game.
