Account and data deletion

You can delete your Kaldera account and all your game data in two ways. Türkçe metin.

In the game

  1. Sign in with Play and enter the world with a character.
  2. Menu → Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Read the warning and type your nickname to confirm (once Google sign-in arrives, you will be asked to re-verify with Google instead).
  4. Tap Delete. If you are in a party, leave it first; if you are in a duel, finish it first.

Your account is closed at once and cannot be played. If you sign in again with the same nickname within 7 days, you are offered Restore account and everything comes back as it was. After 7 days the account is permanently deleted and cannot be restored.

By e-mail (if you uninstalled the app or cannot sign in)

Send an e-mail to nurullahmencik42@gmail.com with the subject "Kaldera account deletion", your nickname and the class and level of one of your characters. You may be asked a short question to confirm the account is yours. Requests are completed within 30 days and confirmed by e-mail.

What is deleted

The account, the nickname, up to 3 characters, all items and gear, storage, gold, quests, waystones, skill ranks, duel wins/losses and ratings.

What is kept for a while

  • Backups: copies of the account file are removed automatically within 60 days. A backup is restored only after a server failure; an account deleted earlier is then deleted again.
  • Server logs: event lines that name your nickname (sign-in, sign-out, character creation, account deletion; no IP addresses) stay until the log file rotates, normally about 2 months, at most 1 year.

Nothing else is kept. Details: Privacy Policy.
