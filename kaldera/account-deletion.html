Account and data deletion

You can delete your Kaldera account (developer: Nurullah Mencik) and all your game data in two ways. Türkçe metin.

In the game

  1. Tap Play, sign in with Google and enter the world with a character.
  2. Menu → Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Read the warning and tap Verify and delete (older versions: Confirm with Google and delete). Google's sign-in window opens: choose the same Google account again. This confirms that you, the owner of the account, are deleting it.
  4. If you are in a party, leave it first; if you are in a duel, finish it first.

Your account is closed at once and cannot be played. If you sign in again with the same Google account within 7 days, you are offered Restore account and everything comes back as it was. After 7 days the account is permanently deleted and cannot be restored.

By e-mail (if you uninstalled the app or cannot sign in)

Send an e-mail to nurullahmencik42@gmail.com with the subject "Kaldera account deletion", your username and the class and level of one of your characters. You may be asked a short question to confirm the account is yours. Requests are completed within 30 days and confirmed by e-mail.

What is deleted

After the 7 days: the account, the username, the link to your Google account (Google's user number), up to 3 characters, all items and gear, storage, gold, quests and daily tasks, waystones, skill ranks and masteries, duel wins/losses and ratings, the friends list and friend requests, chat settings (Terms consent, blocks, mutes), the purchase entitlements (Remove Ads, Premium) with their order numbers, the rewarded-ad limits, your Market listings (the items are deleted with the account) and the Market mailbox (Posta).

Data kept for a while

These records are not part of the account file. They stay until their own retention period ends, then they are removed automatically:

  • Chat and sign-in log (Turkish Law No. 5651): the text of your chat lines, the account number, username, IP address and time of every chat line and sign-in, for 370–400 days from the day they were written.
  • Reports that you made or that concern your account (accounts, usernames, reason, the reported player's last 5 chat lines): until the report is closed, then at most 1 year after closing.
  • Market audit log (listing, purchase, cancel, expiry, collection, removal: account and character number, item, price, time; no IP addresses): at most 1 year.
  • Moderation answers (the results of the developer's moderation commands, which can include a copy of reports): 30 days.
  • Server logs: event lines that name your username (sign-in, sign-out, character creation, purchase checks, account deletion; no IP addresses), until the log file rotates, normally about 2 months, at most 1 year.
  • Backups: copies of the account file are removed within 60 days. A backup is restored only after a server failure; an account deleted earlier is then deleted again.

Deleting your Kaldera account does not delete your Google account and does not refund purchases (refunds follow Google Play's rules); Google keeps its own order records under its policy. To also remove Kaldera's access to your Google account, open myaccount.google.com/connections. Details: Privacy Policy.
